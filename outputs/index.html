A note on privacy

Private by how it works.

Your phrase is used on this device to derive an AES-GCM encryption key. The relay sees room names, connection metadata and encrypted messages, but it never receives the phrase or key.

Calls use direct WebRTC connections protected by DTLS-SRTP. A network relay may forward encrypted media when a direct connection isn’t possible. Compare your phrase out of band and use HTTPS when hosting this app publicly.
